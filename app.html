Method

The contract behind every verdict

What the app does

It fits one straight line to annual means and asks whether the slope is distinguishable from noise. It reports the rate per decade, a 95% interval, the sample size, and the test that produced the verdict.

Statistical contract

  • Annual means only; monthly rows are never fitted.
  • OLS slope with Newey–West HAC standard errors.
  • HAC lag L = max(1, floor(4 · (n/100)2/9)).
  • Verdict at HAC p < 0.05, using the sign of the slope.
  • A verdict also needs the corrected Mann–Kendall test to agree at 5%, in the same direction.
  • 95% interval from the HAC SE with Student-t on n − 2 df.
  • Sen's slope as a robust second opinion.
  • Mann–Kendall with the Hamed–Rao (1998) autocorrelation correction, computed on the Sen-detrended series.
  • Below 20 annual means: exploratory, no significance claim.
  • Map cells use Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction at q = 0.05 across the grid.
  • Neighbouring cells are correlated, so that correction is approximate.
  • Significant means distinguishable from noise at 5%, not large: a trend can pass and still be too small to matter.

Data

  • NASA GISTEMP v4: global gridded temperature anomalies, baseline 1951–1980.
  • Ocean cells carry ERSST-based sea surface temperature; land cells carry surface air temperature. Ocean regions average their cells by area (cos lat) and correct across the eight basins, not the mixed field. The “global ocean” row is the area-weighted mean of those eight basins, so its cells are already counted inside them.
  • NASA POWER: point temperature, rainfall, root-zone wetness and solar irradiance, MERRA-2 derived. POWER answers per 0.5° × 0.625° cell, so neighbouring stations can share one series: 141 station variables are identical to another station's, and a country average can count a cell twice. Twelve stations on the Antarctic and Greenland ice sheets have no root-zone soil moisture at all, that variable is absent there, not zero.
  • Meteomatics: current conditions for every point, proxied server-side so the app itself holds no weather credentials. The build snapshot is labelled and used when the proxy is unavailable.
  • NASA Earthdata: MERRA-2 catalogue access check.
  • NSIDC/NOAA Sea Ice Index (G02135): monthly ice extent per hemisphere, averaged to annual means and served as a timeseries by NOAA PSL.
  • Sentinel-2 L2A: Copernicus multispectral imagery, mosaicked for featured points by Microsoft Planetary Computer.
  • NASA Blue Marble: globe texture and fallback context when a Sentinel-2 mosaic is unavailable; the card label always names the source actually shown.

Featured-point imagery is a low-cloud Sentinel-2 L2A mosaic. The visual asset is true colour TCI (red B04, green B03, blue B02; asset band indices 1, 2, 3; uint8 0–255), served by the Planetary Computer mosaic API. Each case card names its actual source, and data/sentinel_report.json records the STAC item, mosaic request statuses and the signed-COG proof.

What it does not do

  • It does not attribute cause.
  • “Not significant” means “not proven”, not “no trend”.
  • Absolute POWER values are not anomalies; GISTEMP is an anomaly.
  • A significant trend is not necessarily a large one.
  • The SI proposes and explains; it never computes a number.
