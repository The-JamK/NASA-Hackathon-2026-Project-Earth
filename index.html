How a case works

Four steps, one claim at a time.

The intake assistant proposes. The statistics engine decides. You get the part you can defend.

  1. 01 · Intake

    Ask a question

    Name a place and a change — “Is the Sahel getting drier?” The assistant proposes the variable, the place and the time range, and cites the source it used.

  2. 02 · Evidence

    Annual means only

    The record is assembled from cached NASA data. Monthly rows are never fitted, and the annual series is drawn next to the fit so you can see the scatter behind the line.

  3. 03 · Forensics

    Fit and test

    OLS with Newey–West HAC standard errors, a 95% interval from the same errors, and Sen’s slope as a robust second opinion. Mann–Kendall is reported next to the verdict, not used to set it.

  4. 04 · Verdict

    Say what it is

    Significant increase, significant decrease, not proven, or cold case. The rate, the interval, the sample size and the limits sit beside the stamp.

Read it in this order

  • The stamp is the verdict. “Open and shut” means the slope cleared the test with room to spare.
  • The rate is per decade, and the interval is the 95% range for that rate — not for the raw years.
  • n is the count of annual means. Below 20 the app refuses a significance claim and files the case as exploratory.
  • The p-value comes from the HAC test on the slope. A steep-looking line that fails it is not a trend here.

Why you can trust it

The contract behind every verdict.

  • The AI proposes and explains; it never computes. Every figure on a case file comes from code.
  • Annual means only, fitted with OLS and Newey–West HAC standard errors. The verdict is set at HAC p < 0.05, using the sign of the slope.
  • Sen’s slope is a second opinion. Mann–Kendall is descriptive only, so it cannot promote a case the HAC test would not.
  • Map cells must survive a Benjamini–Hochberg correction at q = 0.05 across the grid, so one lucky cell is not called a trend.
  • Under 20 annual means the case is labelled exploratory. There is no causal attribution, and “not significant” means “not proven”, not “no trend”.
